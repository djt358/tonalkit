Here is exactly what happens to what you record.

## What we record

- Your voice reading about 70 short cards. Only your last take of each card is kept.
- Three quick answers about you: how you'd describe yourself as a Mandarin speaker, which Mandarin you grew up hearing, and how you like to see the cards. You can choose **Prefer not to say** on the first two.
- Your phone model and microphone settings, to check audio quality.
- No name, email or phone number. Recordings carry a random six-character code, not your name.

## Why

DJ is building Bendy, a Mandarin-learning app DJ plans to sell, and tonekit, the open-source library that checks its tones. Your recordings test whether the checker works for real voices. DJ will listen to some to check the results.

## Who gets it

- DJ, who receives the file you send.
- It is analysed on DJ's own computers and in a private cloud workspace.
- Nobody else. Nothing leaves your phone until you tap Share.
- We may publish overall results, such as how often the checker was right. Never your recordings.

## What we will never do

- Publish your recordings.
- Share them with anyone else, or send them to any other service.
- Use them to identify you.
- Clone or imitate your voice.

## How long we keep it

Until the project no longer needs it, or until you ask us to delete it. To ask, send DJ your code (you get it at the end, and it's in the file's name). We then delete your recordings, your answers and everything made from them. Overall results already written up can't be redone, but they contain no audio.

## Your choice

Taking part is voluntary. Skip any card, or stop any time. Closing this page sends nothing.
